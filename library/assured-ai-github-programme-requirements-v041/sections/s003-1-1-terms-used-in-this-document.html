1.1 Terms used in this document

TermMeaning
PM & Architecture IntegrationThe programme manager role for the project. Referred to as "the PM"
Technical Integration LeadThe person who confirms that technical components are ready to integrate and validates them (DP-05)
Workstream (WS1 to WS5)The five delivery streams of the project, each with its own lead. "Platform" covers shared hosting and engineering
Epic codeThe reference for each programme epic, for example WS1-E2 (workstream 1, epic 2)
Gates outputA contractual output of the Gates Foundation grant, due in a given month
Research questions (R1 to R7)The seven questions the project is designed to answer
RoutesThe four ways of producing the same product that the project compares: Control (today's human process), Route 0 (an AI model alone), Route A (model plus authoring skills), Route B (model plus skills plus the knowledge graph)
Pipeline stagesThe steps from WHO guidance to finished products: L1 source guidance, KG knowledge graph, L2 operational content (DAK, the digital adaptation kit), L3 computable content (FHIR and CQL), L5 health skills for AI agents (served through MCP), EVAL evaluation, LOC localization for a country
Thin sliceA small piece of content taken through every pipeline stage to prove the end-to-end flow
D-numberA programme dependency record, D01 onward
DEC-numberA programme decision record, DEC-001 onward
Delivery healthWhether an item is On track, At risk or Blocked
SME sign-offReview by a WHO subject-matter expert, for example from the HIV or immunization programme
Validation packetThe evidence pack submitted for review of a produced artefact
Science Division publication clearanceWHO's internal clearance before anything is published
