7.3 Labels

Create these in every repository listed in section 4, except aai-pm. Workstream leads should keep them unchanged.

LabelMeaning
rq:R1 to rq:R7Every research question the item contributes to, including the primary one also recorded in the Primary research question field. The labels are the complete record; the field is the main question only
route:control, route:0, route:a, route:bWhich comparison routes the item covers
gates: plus the output number, one per Gates outputEvery Gates output the item contributes to, including the primary one also recorded in the Gates output field. The labels are the complete record; the field is the primary output only
thin-slicePart of the end-to-end demo slice
sprint-demoTo be shown at the next fortnightly sprint review
blocked, at-riskRaised by the workstream team on its own items (AUTO-04)
needs-reviewReady for WHO review
validation-exceptionSet by AUTO-10 only
