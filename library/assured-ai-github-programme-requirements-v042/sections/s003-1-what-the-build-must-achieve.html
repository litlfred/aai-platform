1. What the build must achieve

Workstream leads run their own delivery in their own projects. Everyone involved in the programme, including partners and Gates Foundation contacts, sees one shared programme picture: what is planned, when, how healthy delivery is, what depends on what, and what is waiting for WHO review. Only genuinely sensitive material is kept back, in a small restricted repository. The same issue is never copied to make any of this work.

The two principles the agent must follow:

  • Share operational status and delivery impact; keep sensitive reasoning and internal deliberation private.
  • Issue fields hold the shared values; the projects are different views of them.
