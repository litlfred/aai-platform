8.1 Assured AI Programme views

Each view lists its layout, which items it shows, how they are grouped and sorted, and the columns to display.

IDViewLayoutItemsGroup and sortColumns
V1Master programmeTableEpicsGroup by Workstream; sort by Start dateTitle, Programme status, Delivery health, Pipeline stage, Start date, Target date, Gates output, Primary research question, sub-issue progress
V2RoadmapRoadmapEpics and milestone issues with datesGroup by Workstream; zoom to quarterStart date, Target date, Programme status, Delivery health
V3MilestonesTableMilestone issues, plus items with any gates: labelGroup by Gates output; sort by Target date. One saved filter per Gates output on its gates: label, which shows both primary and secondary contributionsTitle, Workstream, Programme status, Delivery health, Target date
V4Research questionsTableResearch question issues, plus items with any rq: labelGroup by Primary research question. One saved filter per research question on its rq: labelTitle, Workstream, Programme status, rq: and route: labels
V5Pipeline stagesBoardEpics, plus anything labelled thin-sliceColumns by Pipeline stage, L1 through LOC, with Cross-cutting / N/A shown separately. Saved filter for label:thin-sliceTitle, Workstream, Programme status, Delivery health
V6Integration readinessTableItems labelled thin-slice, and epics with Integration readiness other than Not assessedGroup by Pipeline stage; sort by Target date. Saved filter "Awaiting validation": Integration readiness is Ready to integrateTitle, Workstream, Integration readiness, assignee, Target date, sub-issue progress
V7Sprint reviewTableItems labelled sprint-demoGroup by WorkstreamTitle, Workstream, Programme status, linked epic
V8Next 30 daysTableOpen epics, milestones, dependencies and cross-workstream deliverables with Target date in the next 30 daysGroup by Workstream; sort by Target dateTitle, Workstream, Programme status, Delivery health, assignee, Target date
V9DependenciesTableDependency issuesGroup by Workstream (the waiting workstream); sort by Target dateTitle (starts with D-number), Workstream, Delivery health, Impact, Target date, assignee
V10Blocked and at riskTableDelivery health At risk or Blocked, or label blocked, at-risk or validation-exceptionGroup by Delivery health, then Workstream; sort by Impact, then Target dateTitle, Workstream, Risk category, Impact, Target date, assignee
V11WHO reviewBoardItems with Review type other than Not neededColumns by Review status; sort by Target dateTitle, Workstream, Review type, Target date
V12DecisionsTableDecision issuesGroup by open or closed; sort by Target dateTitle (starts with DEC-number), Workstream, Programme status, Target date
V13OverdueTableTarget date before today and Programme status not Done or CancelledGroup by WorkstreamTitle, Target date, Delivery health, assignee

Notes:

  • V6 answers "are we ready to integrate it?", while V5 answers "where is the work?". The "Awaiting validation" filter is the Technical Integration Lead's work queue.
  • V7 depends on workstream items labelled sprint-demo being added to the project automatically (AUTO-01). After each review the workstream lead removes the label.
  • V10 exists so workstreams can act, not just see. Every item in it must carry a "Next action" line in its issue body (section 7.1). The view description tells readers to open the item for it.
  • In V11, Science Division items appear only when Review type is Science Division publication clearance.
  • V12 makes the DEC issues the decision record from the build onward. Decisions made before the build stay in the PM's existing decision log, and the agent does not import them.
